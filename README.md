# 📊 INTUNE Retail Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This dashboard was developed using real-world retail transaction data provided directly by the Store Manager of **INTUNE TGV Mall**. The objective was to transform raw point-of-sale data (January - July 2026) into a comprehensive, interactive dashboard. 

I delivered this final analysis back to the store management team to assist them in data-driven decision-making, specifically focusing on inventory optimization, customer demographic targeting, and staff allocation during peak revenue days.

## 💡 Key Business Insights Delivered
Based on the analysis provided to the store manager, several key operational insights were identified:

* **Overall Performance Metrics:** Analyzed over 10K transactions resulting in 1.33M units sold, maintaining a healthy overall profit margin of 0.36 and an average bill value of 1.34K.
* **Peak Sales Optimization:** Identified Sundays and Saturdays as the overwhelming drivers of weekly revenue, providing a data-backed rationale for increasing weekend floor staff and inventory readiness.
* **Category Dominance:** Pinpointed "Apparel Men" and "Kids Boys" as the highest-grossing subcategories. 
* **Inventory Focus:** Drill-down analysis revealed that T-Shirts, Shirts, and Jeans are the top three revenue-generating products (accounting for over 15% of total revenue combined), indicating where supply chain efforts should be heavily concentrated.
